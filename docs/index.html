How it works

Ask

Use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Manus like normal. A small slipper shows up beside each answer.

Slap

Click it. A voice lets the AI have it, in your language, at the intensity you picked. Click again to stop.

Escalate

Hold the slipper and scream into your mic. Or record your own rant with My Voice and use that instead.

It never phones home.

SlopSlappy doesn't read, store or send your chats. It asks for the two smallest Chrome permissions there are, draws a button on the page you're looking at, and plays audio that's already on your disk.

Hold-to-scream measures how loud you are and throws it away. My Voice saves to your browser only after you press Save, and is gone when you uninstall.
