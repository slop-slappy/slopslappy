# Changelog

## 1.6.2

- ChatGPT layout compatibility: answer-action discovery now works from
  button/icon metadata (Copy, Read aloud, Retry, Regenerate, votes) even
  when the page has no assistant-role markup. User-marked controls, forms,
  composers and sidebars are excluded. No answer text is inspected.
- Manual placement: when auto-detection can't match, settings show Floating
  mode and a **Place beside answer…** button. Click it, then click an action
  icon under the AI's reply. Escape cancels.
- New default: Punjabi / ਪੰਜਾਬੀ, Man, Clear at 1×, 60 seconds. Nothing plays
  until the slipper is clicked.
- Audio, placement logic, My Voice, permissions and storage keys unchanged
  from 1.6.0.

## 1.6.0

- Small launcher control beside the answer-action row, with corner fallback.
- 12 languages, Man / Woman voices, three intensity tiers, effects, personas,
  hold-to-scream and My Voice.
